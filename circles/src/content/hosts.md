# Host a show

Some of the best concerts happen in living rooms, gardens and cafés after closing. Peerify helps you open your space for an intimate show, with an audience that’s there to listen.

## Three kinds of spaces

**Venues** are places built for live music, with a stage, sound and lights.

**Pop-up spaces** are cafés after hours, shops, community centres and rooms you can hire: not made for gigs, but able to hold a listening audience.

**Home & private spaces** are living rooms, gardens, garages and shared co-op spaces. For home hosts, your address and identity are never shown publicly on Peerify.

## How the host pilot works

1. Tell us about your space: what kind it is, roughly how many people it holds, and where it is.
2. We connect you with artists who are touring, or who fans have pledged to bring, near you.
3. You and the artist agree the terms. You might take a share of the ticket takings, pay the artist a fixed fee and run the door yourself, sell food and drinks, or simply host for the love of it. All of these are fine.
4. You host the show together with the artist and their local crew.
5. Afterwards, everyone reviews everyone, which builds trust for the next show.

## The listening rule

Peerify shows are for audiences who came to hear the artist. That’s why we welcome a café after closing, but not background music for diners.

## What Peerify does, and what it doesn’t

During the pilot, Peerify helps with visibility, introductions to artists, and guidance on running a good show. The event itself is yours: you’re responsible for it, including any local rules on licensing, alcohol, noise and safety where you live.
